# How to Get This On Your Phone (5 minutes)

No terminal needed. Do this all from github.com in your browser.

---

## Step 1: Create the repo

1. Go to **github.com** and log in
2. Click the **+** button (top right) → **New repository**
3. Name it: `pnl-tracker`
4. Keep it **Public** (required for free GitHub Pages)
5. Click **Create repository**

---

## Step 2: Upload the files

1. On your new empty repo page, click **"uploading an existing file"** link
   (or click Add file → Upload files)
2. Drag ALL of these files into the upload area:
   - `index.html`
   - `manifest.json`
   - `sw.js`
   - `icon-192.png`
   - `icon-512.png`
3. Click **Commit changes** (green button at bottom)

---

## Step 3: Turn on GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repo → **Settings** tab (top menu bar)
2. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**
3. Under "Source", select **Deploy from a branch**
4. Branch: select **main**, folder: **/ (root)**
5. Click **Save**
6. Wait 1-2 minutes. Refresh the page.
7. You'll see a green banner with your URL:
   `https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/pnl-tracker`

---

## Step 4: Add to your iPhone

1. Open that URL in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Name it whatever you want → tap **Add**
5. Done. It's on your home screen like a real app.

---

## That's it!

- Opens fullscreen, no browser bar
- Works offline after first load
- Your data saves on your phone (localStorage)
- Nobody else can see your data
- To update the app later, just upload new files to the same repo

---

## Android version

Same steps 1-3, then:
1. Open the URL in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots)
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**
